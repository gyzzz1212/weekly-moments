## 完整提示词

Use case: identity-preserve.
Asset: four-frame transparent PNG character atlas for a calm productivity app, exactly 2 columns x 2 rows, 1024x1536 total, each cell 512x768.
Reference: existing chibi boy, preserve identity: silver lightly lavender parted hair, short gray-black eyes, rounded face, small black earring, black zip jacket with very restrained cyan edging, black trousers.
Change pose and expression substantially: STANDING UPRIGHT with feet on ground, shoulders level, HEAD STRAIGHT, direct level gaze. Relaxed neutral closed mouth, eyebrows neutral. BOTH ARMS NATURALLY DOWN, hands visible beside hips. No hand on chin, no hand in pocket, no leaning, no head tilt, no flirting, no waving on entry, no smile/smirk. Clean quiet friendly practical posture. Simplify hair and highlights, no glowing pink neon, no seductive styling.
Four cells tightly registered with identical head and body scale/position:
top-left neutral upright eyes open, arms at sides;
top-right exact same pose, both eyes closed for blink;
bottom-left exact same upright pose and level head, right hand very slightly opened at hip (small calm acknowledgment, below waist);
bottom-right same body and hand placement as bottom-left, fingers only slightly relaxed.
Moderate chibi proportions, head about one third of body height rather than oversized baby head. Full body visible with small margin, plain transparent alpha background in all four cells, no furniture, no interface panels, no text or marks. Elegant soft clean anime linework.
